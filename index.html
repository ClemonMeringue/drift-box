Make a map

Build it on the middle tile heading south and press Save. Save only keeps it as your project, so you can work on it as long as you like. When it is ready, press Publish next to it below. Changing a published map clears its old times.

All maps

Seasons

New seasons start as a draft. Admins can add and build tracks in any season; only an owner can publish, unpublish or delete. Track marks: ○ to be built, ◐ building (saved but not finished), ● built.

Medal times

Author time in seconds for each track. Gold (+10%), silver (+30%) and bronze (+60%) are worked out from it.

Leaderboard times

Reset

Each button asks you to type a confirmation phrase, like "reset times". This cannot be undone. Your owner account and Season 1 always stay.
